# Style Guide for _Vital Sparks_

Inspired by the Style Guide for the Sacred Harp Publishing Company.

## General Style Guide

Use a recent edition of _The Chicago Manual of Style_ as the basic style guide, with exceptions and amplifications listed here.

## Songs in _The Sacred Harp_

Refer to songs in _The Sacred Harp_ by their tune names and page numbers. Place the page numbers in parentheses following the tune name. When referring to a song more than one time in a passage of text, include the page number only in the first reference. Example: MESSIAH (131t).

### Song titles

Markdown does not natively support small caps; thus, use all caps for tune names (The SHPC Style guide recommends small caps and title case).

### Song page numbers

When referencing the page number on which a song appears in The Sacred Harp, represent the number using the numerals that refer to the page on which the song begins. Page numbers should be appended with a “t” for “top” or “b” for “bottom” if a song is one of two songs that begins on the page in question. The “b” designation should not be used to reference a song that begins on the bottom of a page on which another song that began on a previous page ends.

### Song lyrics

Unless the essay specifically mentions other style uses, use the poetry as found in the 2025 edition of _The Sacred Harp_, which can be found at https://github.com/Sacred-Harp-Publishing-Company/2025-edition.

## Names and Terms

Use the term “Sacred Harp singing” to describe singing from _The Sacred Harp_ and associated practices and traditions. Reserve the term “shape-note singing” for describing the use of shape notes in singing or singing irrespective of the source or shape-note system used. Capitalize “Sacred” and “Harp” but do not use italics when referring to Sacred Harp singing. Reserve italics for references to the editions of the tunebook itself.

## Editions of _The Sacred Harp_

Use the term _The Sacred Harp_ to refer to the tunebook when referring to the tunebook generically irrespective of its edition or publisher. Italicize this and all other titles of books.

The term _The Sacred Harp_ may also be used to refer to the current edition of the tunebook. The current edition may also be referred to by its name in the table below.

Use the following terms to refer to the editions of _The Sacred Harp_ published by the Sacred Harp Publishing Company. When referring to a book for the first time in a document, use the full title. For successive mentions, or when differentiating the edition from other editions of The Sacred Harp, use the edition only. When indicating when the book was published, use the year published.
