# Artificial intelligence policy

How I use AI to support my writing

I would like to be clear about how I use artificial intelligence technology while writing my columns for *Vital Sparks*. Some people know that I have a Ph.D. in computer science with a specialization in artificial intelligence. My doctorate is quite old these days, I studied what is now technically called “GOFAI;” that is, “Good, old-fashioned artificial intelligence.” AI is changing so rapidly, and AI is changing so many things.

## What I use AI for

I use AI for the following purposes:

1. Researching the background to songs and their texts. I fact-check the research before using it.
2. Suggesting songs I might review on a particular topic; for example, I might ask an AI model to make a list of all the songs in *The Sacred Harp Cooper edition, 2012*, that mention angelic beings: the page number, the title, and a reason why. I have created AI skills specific to the 2025 edition and the *Cooper* book for this — which are available to anyone’s use for the asking.
3. Proofing, copyediting, and fact-checking an article after I write it. To be honest, I would rather use a human editor for this, and readers often point out errors I’ve made. I am always grateful for this.
4. Creating thumbnail portraits of existing photos, and potentially editing photos that are used. Substantial edits will be acknowledged as created by AI, but I haven’t done any of this as yet.

## What I do not use AI for

1. I DO NOT use AI to write any of the essay.
2. I DO NOT use generative AI for image creation unless I am doing so for some very specific reason, which will always be disclosed.

If you have any questions about this policy, I would be glad to receive them, and update as necessary.
