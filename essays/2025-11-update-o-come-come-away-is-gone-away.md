# Update: “O Come, Come Away” is gone away

New information has come to light

Thanks to a vibrant discussion on Fasola Discussions group and inter-library loan, I found out a lot of new information about the poetry behind 344 O COME AWAY in the 1991 edition (but which was removed in the 2025 edition). If you found the original essay interesting, you might like to see the updated version: “[O Come, Come Away” is gone away.](https://vitalsparks.beehiiv.com/p/vital-sparks-o-come-come-away-is-gone-away)
